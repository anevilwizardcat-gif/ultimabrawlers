Overview:
--------------
M.Bison, or "Vega" inside Japan, is influenced mainly from the Alpha series. He has some
elements from the VS series, but nothing too major.

I'm not going for something -super- accurate. I'm not a creator like NeoGouki, Sander or Tenshin. I'll get too bored of making
a character. =P
