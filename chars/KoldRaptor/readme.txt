L.Raptor (Zabel) originally by Zelgadis296, edited by Koldskool

Movelist:
Specials:
Skull Punish: HCB, P
Hell's Gate: HCF, K
Death Coper: QCB, K (also usuable in air)
Skull Sting: QCF, K (also usuable in air)

Supers (consume 1 level):
Hell Dunk: QCFx2, P
Ultimate Undead (Dark Force): QCBx2, P
Death Voltage: QCBx2, K (also usuable in air)

MAX Super (consumes 3 levels):
Evil Scream: QCFx2, KK

Command Normals:
Crouch Walk: Down+Forward or Down+Back
Skull Javelin: (when opponent is down) Up+P
Wasp Sting: (in air) Down+K
Extended Reach: Forward+P or K

Others:
Dodge: LP+LK
Roll: Forward+LP+LK
Power Charge: Hold MP+MK
Counterattack: (While guarding) HP+HK (consumes 1 level)
Super Jump: Quickly press Down, then Up. Pressing Up while dashing will also perform a Super Jump.
