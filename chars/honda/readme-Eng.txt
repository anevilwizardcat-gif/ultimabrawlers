  Controls
  --------

    Basic Controls
    --------------
      D - down
      F - forward
      U - up
      B - back
      x - button X (light punch)
      y - button Y (medium punch)
      z - button Z (strong punch)
      a - button A (light kick)
      b - button B (medium kick)
      c - button C (strong kick)

    Standard Movement
    -----------------
      U              - Jump
      UF             - Jump forwards
      F              - Walk forwards
      DF             - Crouch
      D              - Crouch
      DB             - Crouch or low guard
      B              - Walk backwards or high guard
      UB             - Jump back
      F, F           - Forward dash
      B, B           - Back dash
      x+y/x+z/y+z/a+b/b+c/a+c             - Recovery (when falling)
       
    Basic Standing Attacks
    ----------------------
      x              - L. Punch
      y              - M. Punch
      z              - S. Punch
      a              - L. Kick
      b              - M. Kick
      c              - S. Punch
      F + b         - Knee Kick
      F + c         - Blow Kick 

    Basic Crouching Attacks
    -----------------------
      x              - L. Punch
      y              - M. Punch
      z              - S. Punch
      a              - L. Kick
      b              - M. Kick
      c              - S. Punch
      DF + z       - Low Blow

    Basic Air Attacks
    -----------------
      x              - L. Punch
      y              - M. Punch
      z              - S. Punch
      a              - L. Kick
      b              - M. Kick
      c              - S. Punch

    Throws
    ------
      F/B + x+y/y+z/x+z                  - Punch Throw
      F/B + a+b/b+c/a+c                  - Kick Throw
      (in the air) F/B + x+y/y+z/x+z  - Air Throw

    Special Attacks
    ---------------
      (during guard) F + same power PK  - Zero Counter 

    [Normal Groove]
      B (hold 1second) F + P              - Super Zutsuki

      F, D, DF + K                              - Super HyakkanOtoshi  

      Lever 1 Round (360) + P            - OoichouNage  

      press P quickly                         - Hyakkuretzjyou    

    [G-Groove]
      (in the air) D + M.K                    - TenmaKuujinkyaku

      (in the air) D, DF, F + P              - ZankuuZutsuki

      B (hold 1second) F + P             - GouZutsuki

      F, D, DF + P                             - ShouZutsuki

      Lever 1 Round (360) + P           - MetsatsuOoichouNage  

      press P quickly                        - GouHyakkuretzjyou  

      F, D, DF + 3P or 3K                   - AshuraSenkuu
      B, D, DB + 3P or 3K
 
      F, D, DF + K                             - HyakkiShuu
     
     ¦¦ No input                               - HyakkiGouZan
     
     ¦¦ P                                         - HyakkiGouShou

     ¦¦ K                                         - HyakkiGouSen

     ¦¦ (near opponent)F or B + P     - HyakkiGouSai

     ¦¦ (near opponent)F or B + K     - HyakkiGouTsui


    Hyper Attacks
    -------------
      
      D, DB, B, D, DB, B + S              - G-Groove starting/cancel

      D, DF, F, D, DF, F + P               - OniMusou(MetsatsuGouZutsuki)
 
      D, DF, F, D, DF, F + K               - FujiOroshi

      F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B + P        - OrochiKudaki (3 level only)

      (when G-Groove)L.P, L.P, F, L.K, S.P     - ShunGokuJyou (3 level only)
