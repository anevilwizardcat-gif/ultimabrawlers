--------------
HOW TO INSTALL
--------------

Unzip the archive into a sub-directory of your MUGEN
directory called "chars\SFA3Guile" and add the line
"sfa3guile/guile.def" to your select.cfg under 
the character section.

Move
----

- Sonic Hurricane : charge-back, fwd, back, fwd, x/y/z

- Sonic Mission : charge-back, fwd, a+b or b+c
- Sonic Jet : QCF x2 ,x/y/z

- Zero Counter2 : during a guard hit, QCF,a/b/c
- Sonic Boom : charge-back, fwd, x/y/z 
- Somersault : charge-down, up, a/b/c
- Downersault: (air) charge up, down, a/b/c
- Dunk: (close) QCB + y
- Heaven Drop: (close) QCB + z
