SQUALL LEONHART 
by SeanAltly
Christian Sekhanan as the voice of Squall Leonhart

****Details***

-4 Button System (LP,HP,LK,HK)
-Loose chain combo system, for example:
 -LP > HP > C.HP (Launcher) > A.LP > A.HP
 -LK > HK > Gunblade Rush > Gunblade Trigger
 -LP > LP > F+LP > HP > C.HP (Launcher) > A.LK > A.HK > A.HP or Fated Circle
 -LP > LP > HP > C.HP (Launcher) > Gunblade Rush > Renzokuken > Lion Heart
 -There's tons others, but you get the idea
-Back Hop, Run, Super RUn Jump, Air Dash

**Unique tactic**

-None yet, but the "S" button is still used for Supers.

***Command Moves***

-Right Straight - F+LP

-Hopping Slash - F+HP

***Special Moves***

-Gunblade Rush - D,DF,F,P
 -Gunblade Trigger - Press P again when Gunblade Rush hits

-Rough Divide (EX) - F,D,DF,P

-Magic: Blizzard (EX) - D,DB,B,P (LP for close, HP for far)

-Magic: Thunder (EX) - D,DB,B,K (LK for close, HK for far)

-Fated Circle (EX) - D,DF,F,P in mid-air

***Level 1 Super Moves***

-Magic: Blizzaga - D,DB,B,S

-Magic: Thundaga - B,D,DB,S

-Blasting Zone - F,D,DF,S
 
-Renzokuken - D,DF,F,S
 -Renzokuken Trigger - Press P again each time Renzokuken hits, up to 7 times
 
***Level 3 Super Move***

-Lion Heart - D,DF,F,S during Renzokuken (between 1st and 7th hit)
