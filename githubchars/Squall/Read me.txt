;==================================================
;How To Combo
;==================================================

Basic (Punch, Kick, Sword, etc.) - Lv.1
Special - Lv.2
Super - Lv.3

Basic > Basic
Basic > Special
Basic > Super
Special > Special
Special > Super

Lv.1 > Lv.1
Lv.1 > Lv.2
Lv.1 > Lv.3
Lv.2 > Lv.2
Lv.2 > Lv.3

You can't chain everything. Try experimenting.
