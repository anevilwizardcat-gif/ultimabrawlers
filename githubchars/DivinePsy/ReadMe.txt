---------------<Character Info>------------------

(**Note: 1-10 Ranking System: 1 = Lowest, 10 = Best)

 - Intelligence: 7
 - Strength: 5
 - Speed: 8
 - Stamina: 4
 - Energy Projection: 5
 - Fighting Ability: 8

The alluring psychic ninja of Marvel Comics "X-Men", Psylocke. Being a ninja and female to boot, it's pretty much a given-
that she'd be extremely fast and agile. Just as agile as Mai or Chun-Li. Being a mutant with powers increases her attack-
power quite a bit though, in comparison to most female fighters. Though, just like them, she's very much lacking in the
endurance/stamina department... As such, you should know the routine. Utilize her speed/agility to combo your way to victory.

----------------<Move Details>-------------------

 - Spike Heel is an overhead, except when command cancelled
 - Hell Wheel(air version) can catch foes in their hit states and isn't affected by the juggle system
 - Psylocke's taunt is a attack(though not a very effective/useful one =P)
 - (Air)Psi-Blast's trajectory differs depending on which punch button you use to perform it.
   LP goes at a downward angle, MP(EX) goes straight, HP goes at a upward angle
 - Air LP Psi-Blast can otg foes
 - EX ground Psi-Blade can be chained up to 3 times by pressing any kick during the respective chains
 - EX (Air) Psi-Drill can resist normal/special projectiles
 - Ninjutsu is a teleport tech that puts Psylocke in various positions, depending on which button used-
   to perform it. Punches put Psylocke in the air, while kicks keep her grounded.
 - Air (EX)Psi-Drill can ONLY be perform during Ninjutsu's air variation, as Psylocke falls to the ground
 - Grounded (MAX)Psi-Thrust, Kochou Gakure, and Psi-Maelstrom are unblockable for airbourne foes
 - You can redirect the direction of MAX Psi-Thrust by holding the opposite direction during the move
 - Mash buttons during Psi-Maelstrom to maximize it's damage! 
   
---------------<Gameplay Notes>------------------

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Most Normal standing/crouching attacks can be canceled into Special and Super moves
 - All Normal air attacks are Special and Super cancellable
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Performing a Super/MAX Cancel resets the juggle points but also reduces damage dealt
   
MAX MODE:
 - Your attack and defense is increased while in MAX Mode
 - EX moves can be performed at no power cost, though take away from time sustained in MAX mode
 - All Lv1 supers can be performed at no power cost, though immediately uses up all time in MAX mode
 - All Lv2 and higher supers will cost 1 power bar, along with using up all time in MAX mode
 - Even without a power bar, as long as you have time left in MAX mode, you can perform Lv1 supers/EX moves
 - You can not Power Charge/gain power while in MAX mode
 - You can not perform a Zero Counter while in MAX Mode
